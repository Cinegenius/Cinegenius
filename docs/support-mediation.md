# Optional support-mediated rentals

User-confirmed policy, 11 November 2026:

- Ordinary self-service registration, profiles, listings, search and direct contact remain currently free.
- Only an expressly agreed, successful rental mediation by CineGenius Support earns the 5% share. Ordinary support contact or platform use never authorizes a paid mandate.
- Basis: the agreed total rental price including VAT. The share is included in the renter's payment, retained at settlement, and does not increase that total. The explicit confirmed example is EUR1,000 paid, EUR50 retained for CineGenius, EUR950 to the provider.
- Additional services may have a higher individually agreed total fee. Scope and fee must be expressly agreed before commissioning; no unilateral or retroactive increase.
- CineGenius is in its start-up/development phase, with services expanded gradually.

This change describes the optional service in all nine catalogs, adds one shared server-rendered notice to pricing/about/terms, qualifies former unconditional no-commission claims, and links help/request screens to the explanation. Existing German-authoritative legal disclosure remains in place; translations are not represented as separately lawyer-approved documents. Terms display date updated to11November2026.

No payment collection, charge, Stripe activation, VAT computation, provider payout, support mandate storage, booking API, existing agreement or existing record is changed. The current request API continues to store platform_fee0; the notice does not claim that the website processes a settlement. Actual engagements require an express prior agreement and a separately agreed settlement method. The tax treatment and invoicing of the retained share must be handled in that agreement/accounting; the confirmed gross allocation must not be silently increased by adding tax on top of the renter's displayed total.

The unused historical commission-tier helper is not enabled or repurposed. Historical legal-review drafts are not overwritten. Release uses the normal clean-main guard and native GitHub Desktop source-control workflow.
